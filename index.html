🏰 Castle Strategy 🏰

Inspired by Anakin's Castle Duels

A 2D real-time strategy game where two castles battle against each other! Defend your castle and destroy the enemy by producing units, managing resources, and casting powerful spells.

⚔️ 6 Unit Types

Knights, Archers, Spearmen, Mages, Cannons, and Heroes!

💰 Resource Management

Generate gold and build mines to increase income

✨ Spell System

Cast Fireball, Heal, Gold Boost, and Freeze spells

🤖 AI Opponent

Battle against an adaptive AI with multiple strategies

🎮 Multiplayer 1vs1 NOW AVAILABLE! Play online directly in your browser! Press 2 in-game to start matchmaking. Requires a deployed server on Railway.

🎮 Controls

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Click on the game canvas first to enable keyboard controls!

  • 1-6 - Produce units (Knight, Archer, Spearman, Mage, Cannon, Hero)
  • M - Build Mine (+3 gold/sec)
  • F - Cast Fireball spell
  • H - Cast Heal spell
  • G - Cast Gold Boost spell
  • Z - Cast Freeze spell
  • 1 or SPACE - Start Singleplayer
  • 2 - Start Multiplayer 1vs1 Online

💡 Tip: You can also click the on-screen buttons in-game if keyboard doesn't work!
